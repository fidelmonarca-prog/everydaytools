How it works

Reduce file size: creates a smaller image file using compression. Change dimensions: sets a specific width and height in pixels. Convert format: saves a photo as JPG, PNG or WebP. Compression results vary by photo and format; files are not guaranteed to become smaller.

Your privacy

Your images stay in your browser and are not sent to our servers. Animated images are exported as a still frame. Transparent areas become white when saving to JPG. Very large files may exceed your device's memory.
